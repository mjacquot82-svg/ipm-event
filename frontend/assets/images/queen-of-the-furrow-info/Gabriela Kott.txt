HALDIMAND COUNTY – Gabriela Kott is proudly representing Haldimand County. She is from West Lincoln, Ontario. She is entering her third year in the Bachelor of Science in Agriculture program, majoring in Animal Science at the University of Guelph. Her goal is to become a livestock veterinarian specializing in bovine health, reproduction, and welfare, allowing her to support farmers and contribute to advancements in the industry.
