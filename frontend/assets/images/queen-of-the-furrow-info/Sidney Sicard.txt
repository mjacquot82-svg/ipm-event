KEPPEL-SARAWAK COUNTY – Sidney Sicard is proudly representing Keppel-Sarawak. She is 20 years old and from Markdale, Ontario. She is currently entering her second year of the Veterinary Technology program at the University of Guelph, Ridgetown Campus. Sidney works as a Direct Support Worker, supporting adults with developmental disabilities. After graduating, she plans to become a Registered Veterinary Technician specializing in large animal medicine while continuing to advocate for Ontario agriculture and remaining actively involved in the plowing community.
