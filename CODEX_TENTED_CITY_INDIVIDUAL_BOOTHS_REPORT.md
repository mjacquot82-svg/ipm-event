## Confirmed defects; implementation unchanged

1. **Phone booth border expands the selected cell.** For ACE at 320px, intended cell width is **7.64px**, but the selected DOM box paints at **18.90px**; at 390px, **9.31px** becomes **18.90px**. StumpedIt's narrower cell measures **6.95px / 8.46px**, also painting at **18.90px**. The selected cell uses a 3px border on each side before the 3.15× map transform; its minimum border box exceeds the percentage cell width and overlaps the next booth. Thick vendor/parent outlines also make the phone emphasis visually broad. The intended geometry center remains on screen, but this is not exact visual precision. [320px ACE](release-artifacts/tented-city-proof-20260910/preview-browser/screenshots/320-vendor-1A-09.png), [390px StumpedIt](release-artifacts/tented-city-proof-20260910/preview-browser/screenshots/390-vendor-5A-33.png), [desktop GGS](release-artifacts/tented-city-proof-20260910/preview-browser/screenshots/1440-vendor-2B-23.png).
2. **Reset control is behind the selection card.** Browser hit-testing at its center reaches the card or Dismiss button at all widths; normal click/tap times out. Reset works after the card is cleared, independently verified from a wheel-zoomed state.
3. **Mouse drag can trigger selection on release.** From Harkness, a 45×35px drag changes the query to `RV` (320px/desktop) or `SECOND STREET` (390px), with camera scale changing from 3.15 to the newly selected region's zoom. Single-touch drag passed, but desktop drag does not preserve the selected vendor. [Gesture measurements](release-artifacts/tented-city-proof-20260910/gesture-followup.json).

These findings are reported, not silently fixed, because the requested scope was to finish proof/preview against the pushed implementation.
