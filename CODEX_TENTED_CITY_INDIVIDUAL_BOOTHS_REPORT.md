## Fixes verified

1. **Highlight overlap fixed.** The selected booth now keeps its layout cell and draws the 3px gold outline in an inset child overlay. The selected background remains inside the booth geometry, the parent range remains readable, and all phone painted-width checks pass.
2. **Reset fixed.** Reset has a higher stacking level than the info card and uses a dedicated reset action that clears vendor, semantic parent, booth, query, unavailable state, and camera. Vendor A → reset → vendor B → reset passed at all widths.
3. **Mouse drag fixed.** A moved pointer suppresses the synthesized click at the viewport capture boundary while preserving ordinary clicks. Desktop drag now changes translation without selecting a neighboring map hitbox or changing zoom; touch pan and wheel behavior remain passing.
